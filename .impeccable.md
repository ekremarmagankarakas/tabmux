## Design Context

### Users
Keyboard-focused developers organizing Chrome tabs, groups, and saved sessions.

### Brand Personality
Compact, direct, familiar. Preserve the existing tmux-style interface, confirmed by the user.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark neutral background, light monospace text, restrained borders, dense readable lists. Match existing tabmux UI.

### Design Principles
- Put keyboard access and predictable focus first.
- Keep sessions and recovery easy to scan.
- Make failures persistent and actionable.
- Use native controls and accessible labels.
